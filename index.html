About Us

At Shanti Niketan School, we believe that education is not just about academic success—it is about nurturing responsible, confident, creative, and compassionate individuals who are prepared to make a positive difference in the world. Our school provides a safe, supportive, and inspiring learning environment where every student is encouraged to discover their potential, develop their talents, and pursue their dreams.

Welcome to Shanti Niketan Public School

Education is not only about academic success. It is about developing responsible, confident and compassionate individuals who can contribute positively to society.

At Shanti Niketan Public School, we encourage our students to explore their potential, ask questions, think creatively and learn from every experience. We believe that every child is unique and deserves the right guidance and opportunities to grow.

Together with our dedicated teachers and supportive parents, we aim to create a strong foundation for every student's future.

Dr. Abhinav Anand
Principal, Shanti Niketan Public School
